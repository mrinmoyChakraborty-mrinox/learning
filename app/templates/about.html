About This App

This is a Student Attendance Management Web App built with Flask and MySQL. It allows teachers to manage classes, add students, and mark attendance easily. Students can log in to view their attendance records and link their accounts to their class records.

Features:

  • ✅ Teacher dashboard for managing students and attendance
  • ✅ Student dashboard to view attendance and link account
  • ✅ Secure login system with session management
  • ✅ Simple and clean interface

This app was developed as a learning project to practice Flask, MySQL, and web development concepts.
